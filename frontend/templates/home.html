The Four Strongholds of the Protocol

A unified staking and credit environment built on native Bitcoin smart contract conditions.

🔐 1. Youth Survey Staking

Students answer survey research. Instead of direct spendable cash, rewards compile into locked Bitcoin shares that establish a baseline credit score, qualifying them for school fee loans they repay with local currency while their Bitcoin stays untouched.

🏥 2. Behavioral Rehab Escrows

Donors fund recovery paths in Bitcoin. Oracle-tracked milestones verify sobriety metrics. Volatility yield helps clinics manage costs, and patients complete programs with fractionally earned Bitcoin pool shares as a sobriety incentive.

💰 3. Commercial Service Pools

Vendors and enterprises route incoming invoices directly into unspent Master Pool shares. Instead of liquidating Bitcoin for daily fiat costs, vendors earn yield-bearing shares they can use to qualify for business credit lines.

⏳ 4. Narrative Shard Recovery

Eliminate vulnerable seed phrase sheets. Encrypt and reassemble wallet private keys using personal memory experience narrative hashes that are impossible to forget.
